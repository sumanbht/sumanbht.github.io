Research

I am an observational astrophysicist whose research focuses on time-domain stellar astrophysics, stellar variability, and emission-line stars. My work investigates the evolution of circumstellar disks, binary interactions, and transient phenomena in Classical Be stars using large multi-epoch datasets from ground- and space-based observatories such as ESO, TESS, Gaia, LAMOST, and BeSS.

I combine spectroscopy, photometry, machine learning, and statistical analysis to explore stellar evolution. My methodological approach includes advanced statistical data modeling and interpolation techniques—such as integrating Piecewise Cubic Hermite Interpolating Polynomials (PCHIP) with Gaussian Process regression—to analyze complex binned spectral data, particularly focusing on specific iron emission lines of Be stars in the 6000 to 8000 angstrom range. I aim to advance computational techniques for survey data analysis.

Teaching & Mentoring

I am dedicated to academic mentorship and have co-supervised multiple MSc (Physics) students for their Master's dissertations at CHRIST (Deemed to be University). Key supervision topics include:

  • Study of emission line stars.
  • Study of Runaway stars.
  • V/R variation study of Classical Be stars using LAMOST spectral survey.
  • Ha variability of Galactic Classical Be stars.

Additionally, I have served as a resource person supporting students in the Astronomy Astrophysics Introductory Lectures Lab (AAILL-26) at Vainu Bappu Observatory, and I have conducted hands-on sessions for astronomical data processing.

Outreach

I am actively involved in public outreach and science communication. I have delivered more than 70 popular astronomy talks at various schools, colleges, associations, and organizations across India to popularize astronomy.

My involvement also includes acting as a resource person for the ESO Open Day in Santiago, Chile, and participating in numerous astronomy camps and workshops organized by educational institutions and amateur astronomy associations like the Sky Watchers Association and Kolkata Institute of Fundamental Science and Astronomy.

About Me

I am currently a PhD Candidate in Stellar Astrophysics at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bangalore. My doctoral thesis focuses on "Binary interactions and transient circumstellar disks in Be stars: a multi-epoch spectroscopic and photometric study."

During my doctoral studies, I was awarded a prestigious ESO Studentship, allowing me to conduct research at the European Southern Observatory in Vitacura, Chile. I also hold an MPhil in Physics (specialization: astrophysics) from CHRIST University and an MSc in Physics from the University of Kalyani, where my research centered on reconstructing the Hubble Diagram using the Abell catalog.

Outside of academia, I am passionate about cycling, painting, and astrophotography, which allows me to explore and appreciate the beauty of the night sky visually.
